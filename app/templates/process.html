What happens to your file

Three steps, all from src/.

  1. 1
    Clean

    Invalid rows and exact duplicates removed, missing values filled, ADR capped at the training cap of {{ "{:,.0f}".format(features.adr_cap) }}.

  2. 2
    Engineer

    {{ features.engineered | length }} row-wise features added, e.g. total_nights, adr_per_person, previous_cancel_ratio.

  3. 3
    Transform

    The saved preprocessor.joblib selects {{ features.numeric | length + features.binary | length + features.categorical | length }} inputs and encodes them into {{ features.n_outputs }} columns. Unknown categories fall into "infrequent".
